Why is the Philippines losing money?
-

Using the Global Superstore dataset and Tableau, this project investigates why the Philippines has high sales but is the biggest profit drain in the APAC protfolio

The Paradox
-
Sales grew **118%** from 2021 to 2024, yet the market lost money in all four years.

What the Analysis Found
-
1. **Location**: Manila generates ~65% of national revenue but accounts for ~70% of the losses
2. **Products**: Tables and Copiers are the biggest loss makers, as shipping costs wipe out their margins
3. **Pricing**: "Critical" and "High" priority orders get discounts as steep as low-priority ones, so the company absorbs the cost of fast shipping

4. Recommendations
   -
   1. Cap discounts at 15%
   2. Remove discounts on urgent shipping
   3. Review Tables and Copiers within 90 days
   4. Shift focus from Manila to higher-margin cities
